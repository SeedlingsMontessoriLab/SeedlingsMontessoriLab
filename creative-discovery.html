Parent and Me

Parent and Me supports each child as an individual while helping parents understand Montessori principles, child development, and practical ways to create calmer home rhythms.

Individual support
  • Teachers observe each child's interests, temperament, strengths, and challenges.
  • Support may include separation, emotional regulation, language, social interaction, transitions, toilet learning readiness, self-care, coordination, and sensory needs.
  • Families receive short individual feedback, practical suggestions, and home environment guidance after class.
  • The goal is to build confidence, connection, and a more peaceful family rhythm.
8-week parent topics
  • Week 1 Understanding separation and building trust.
  • Week 2 Independence and practical life at home.
  • Week 3 Emotional regulation and respectful responses to big feelings.
  • Week 4 Language development through daily experiences.
  • Week 5 Social development, turn-taking, and conflict resolution.
  • Week 6 Concentration, repetition, and purposeful play.
  • Week 7 Toilet learning readiness and respectful support.
  • Week 8 Montessori at home, calm rhythms, and material rotation.
