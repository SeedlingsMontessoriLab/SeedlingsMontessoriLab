Infant Nido

For children 3-18 months, the Infant Nido treats caregiving as curriculum. Each day is shaped by secure attachment, freedom of movement, individualized feeding and sleep rhythms, and respectful care routines.

Daily rhythm
  • 7:00-8:00 Early drop-off with calm arrival, quiet play, feeding, and diapering as needed.
  • 8:00-8:30 Individual greeting, family communication, breakfast until 8:30, floor play, or rest.
  • 8:00-10:00 Work and exploration with movement, sensory materials, songs, naming, yoga, and snack.
  • 10:00-11:15 Outdoor time for stroller walks, supervised movement, nature observation, and fresh air.
  • 11:15-12:00 Feeding and care routine with lunch, washing, diapering, and a peaceful transition.
  • 12:00-2:30 Individualized rest and sleep in a quiet environment.
  • 2:30-6:00 Wake-up, feeding, soft music, books, floor play, outdoor time, snack, and departure.
Care focus
  • Freedom of movement: crawling, cruising, walking, and supervised floor exploration.
  • Sensory and language development through object permanence materials, songs, and naming.
  • Care routines are integrated respectfully instead of rushed or treated as interruptions.
  • Teachers stay closely aligned with each child's feeding, rest, and transition needs.
